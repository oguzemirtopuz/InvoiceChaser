### Tweet 3 (How it Works)
InvoiceChaser runs on an automated 4-tier escalation model:

1️⃣ **Tier 1 (T-3 days):** Polite, proactive reminder with 1-click payment link.
2️⃣ **Tier 2 (Due Day):** Friendly due-date notification.
3️⃣ **Tier 3 (T+3 / T+5 days):** Firm overdue follow-up.
4️⃣ **Tier 4 (T+7 days):** Formal escalation notice before legal review.

Every email is dynamically generated by Google Gemini AI in Turkish or English based on client preferences.
